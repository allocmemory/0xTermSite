### No release yet

0xTerm is in development, working toward v0.1. There is nothing to
download today.

### What v0.1 will be

v0.1 ships as a DMG: open it, drag 0xTerm to Applications, and launch it
from the Dock. It will be signed ad hoc, not notarized, so the first launch
needs a right-click and **Open** to get past Gatekeeper.

### Follow along

The code lives at
[github.com/allocmemory/0xTerm](https://github.com/allocmemory/0xTerm).
Building it takes Rust and Xcode, because the GPU shaders compile at build
time.
